# Training intelligence

HomeFit records training wherever it happens. Its central question is: given recent sessions, goals and recovery, what would be useful next?

## First increment

Completed sessions in the last seven local calendar days feed an inspectable coverage summary. External sessions have a program/source, user-confirmed focus tags, optional effort (1–10), and reported steps, miles and calories. Program names are searchable recording aids, not automatic evidence of stimulus. Existing logs remain compatible and can be enriched through Edit activity details.

Guided sessions contribute exercise-list exposure; external sessions contribute self-reported exposure. Counts are sessions, not effective sets, adequate weekly doses or readiness scores. Planned and future sessions are excluded. Partial runs with aggregate set counts cannot be assigned to specific movements. This conservative first increment does not prescribe extra training from missing data.

## Program knowledge next

The supplied deep-research report provides the product direction. Its embedded citation identifiers are not independently usable references. Before shipping workout-specific profiles, verify primary program sources and store their URLs, review dates, workout/edition identifiers and confidence.

Model individual sessions within Caroline Girvan EPIC/IRON/FUEL, P90X/P90X3, Body Beast, LIIFT4, INSANITY, StrongLifts, CrossFit, Orangetheory, Peloton, Apple Fitness+ and Les Mills. Never assign all workouts in a brand identical stimulus. Ask users to confirm what they actually did, including modifications. Yoga, Pilates and walking should use the same session model.

## Next increments

1. Persist per-exercise completed sets and sides, load and repetitions; distinguish partial sessions from planned sessions and preserve replay history.
2. Add muscle groups, movement patterns, actual cardio minutes/intensity, impact and confidence to verified workout templates. Do not fabricate precise set equivalents from video duration or brand names.
3. Add soreness by body area, pain distinctions, sleep and fatigue check-ins; use a 48–72 hour recovery window alongside seven-day coverage and 28-day trends.
4. Introduce transparent goal-specific planning for mass, lean, GLP-1 maintenance, endurance, athleticism and strength. Validate targets separately. Let advice choose an outside session, substitution, lighter day or rest rather than always adding a HomeFit workout.
5. Import device measurements with permissions, original units/provenance and duplicate detection. Retain reported calories as estimates from their source.
6. Gym-photo assistance: extract an equipment inventory, have the user confirm uncertain items and available loads, then compose a workout from reusable exercises. No photo recognition or device connections exist in this increment.

The local assistant is still a deterministic scheduling helper. Coverage is local browser data, with no AI inference service or account synchronization.
